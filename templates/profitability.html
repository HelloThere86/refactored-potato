Understanding Your Profitability
  • Direct Expenses: Expenses specifically associated with a single business (e.g., Lettuce Seeds).
  • Farm-Wide / Shared Expenses: Expenses that support the farm as a whole and have not been assigned to a specific business (e.g., General Electricity).
  • Estimated Profit: Revenue minus the expenses currently allocated. (Note: Individual business profits do not currently include farm-wide shared costs, which are only deducted from the Entire Farm total).
